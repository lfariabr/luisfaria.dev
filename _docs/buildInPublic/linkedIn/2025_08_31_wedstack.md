# LinkedIn Post - Wedstack

Date: 2025-08-31

Have you ever imagined being a Software Engineer, Project Manager, Designer, Customer Support and Husband of the main stakeholder (aka the Bride) - all at once? 
Yep, you read that right. 

That was me for the past 4 weeks, during the conception, development, and deployment of Wedstack - a modern wedding invitation app I built from scratch.

Key features:
- Presence confirmation widget
- Guestbook to leave us a message
- Stripe gifts checkout (Apple Pay, PayID, Pix, Cards)
- Multi-language (EN/PT) toggle
- Soft animations and custom components with shadcn

You can try it here: https://weddingln.com
Leave us a message or, if you’re feeling generous, send us a gift ❤️
