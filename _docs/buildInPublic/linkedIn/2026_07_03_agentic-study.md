# LinkedIn Post: Agentic Study

*Date: 2026-07-03*

Ever been handed 9 Deep Learning, Machine Learning and Big Data projects to ship in 12 weeks?

That's the throughput of my second term in a Master's degree.

Flipped-classroom format: all the material is dumped upfront, every chapter, paper, book and resource. 3 subjects in parallel, 100+ resources to digest.

Processing and retaining all of that without a system is not sustainable. So I sharpened my model-agnostic agentic AI pipeline. Claude Code, Codex, Gemini... pick your poison.

How it works:
1. maps each subject from its source PDFs
2. turns resources into cited notes
3. after I read the dense notes, compresses the module into one mental model and a comparison table
4. quizzes me one question at a time, grading the first attempt honestly (a 1.6/5 stings - that's the point)
5. drafts a one-page revision sheet I then rewrite by hand
6. scaffolds issues + quality checks around each assessment

The loop now looks suspiciously like the Deep Learning course itself: raw material is the input, structured notes are the encoding, compression is the bottleneck, active recall is the forward pass, the score is the loss, targeted feedback is the backprop analogy, and rewriting the explanation is the update. The assessment is the unseen test set.

The AI is the accelerator, the learning is mine.

It summarises, compresses, retrieves, measures, and checks. I encounter the detail, decide what matters, answer from memory, hand-write my own understanding, make every academic call, and submit.

1,100+ commits in, this stopped being a study workflow. It's public proof of how I work as an engineer: decompose messy inputs, define contracts, keep it traceable, ship evidence.
