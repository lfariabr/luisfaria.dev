Back in October, I posted about Wedstack, the wedding invitation app I built from scratch for my own big day — mixing Software Engineer, PM, Designer, Support… and Husband of the main stakeholder (aka the Bride 😅).

I open-sourced everything because… why not?

Maybe someone would learn from it, maybe fork it, or maybe it would just sit quietly on GitHub.

Well… it didn’t sit quietly.

This week, I noticed organic traffic hitting my portfolio from a domain I didn’t recognize.
Clicked out of curiosity…

And boom:
A fully customised wedding website built using Wedstack.

Two people I’ve never met will get married in 2026 using the exact app I built for my own wedding.
No messages. No requests. No fanfare.
Just open source doing open-source things: helping someone, somewhere, without you even knowing.

That absolutely made my day.

Moments like this remind me why building in public is so cool.
You ship something with good intentions, and sometimes it finds a life far beyond what you imagined.
You never know who you’re inspiring or empowering.

And to the couple using Wedstack… wishing you an incredible wedding day.
It’s awesome to have even a tiny part of your story built with my code.

Here’s to more creativity, more sharing, and more unexpected wins in 2026. 💙
