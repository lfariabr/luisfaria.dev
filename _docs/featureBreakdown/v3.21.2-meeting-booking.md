# v3.21.2 — Meeting Booking After Payment

## GitHub Issue

**Issue:** [#314](https://github.com/lfariabr/luisfaria.dev/issues/314) (part of epic [#311](https://github.com/lfariabr/luisfaria.dev/issues/311))

**Blocked by:** [#313](https://github.com/lfariabr/luisfaria.dev/issues/313) (native GitHub dependency set)

**Title:** `feat(support): meeting booking after payment (v3.21.2)`

**Summary:**
The meeting product (AUD 150) takes the money but gives the Supporter no way to book the meeting. This ticket adds pay first, then book: after a paid meeting checkout, the Supporter is pointed at a free, hidden Cal.com event type, both on the success page and by email.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `checkoutSessionStatus` returns `productKey`
- [ ] Success page shows "Book your session" only for a paid meeting session; coffee and unpaid sessions do not show it
- [ ] Meeting Supporters receive one booking email (from `contact@luisfaria.dev`) with the Cal.com link, also on webhook replays
- [ ] The Cal.com URL is read from config/env; nothing hardcoded
- [ ] Tests: resolver returns `productKey`, success page button logic, booking email sent once for meeting and never for coffee

---

## Overview

Third of three Support checkout tickets. The meeting stays a Support checkout product (`meeting` key); once it is paid, the Supporter gets a Cal.com booking link on `/payment/success` and in an email sent by the v3.21.1 webhook.

## Problem

1. **Paid, but no way to book.** After paying for a meeting, the Supporter lands on a generic success page and has to email Luis to arrange a time.
2. **Success page can't tell products apart.** `checkoutSessionStatus` in `backend/src/schemas/types/stripeTypes.ts` returns only `sessionId`, `paymentStatus`, `status`, although `createCheckoutSession` already writes `productKey` into session metadata.

## Changes

### 1. `backend/src/schemas/types/stripeTypes.ts`, `backend/src/services/stripe.ts`, `backend/src/resolvers/stripe/queries.ts`
Add `productKey` to `checkoutSessionStatus`, read from session metadata in `getCheckoutSessionStatus`.

### 2. `backend/src/config/config.ts`, `backend/.env.example`
New `calMeetingUrl` from `CAL_MEETING_URL`. Not hardcoded anywhere.

> **Decided:** `checkoutSessionStatus` returns a nullable `bookingUrl`, set only when the session is paid and `productKey=meeting`. The URL lives in one backend env var (`CAL_MEETING_URL`, also passed to the `api` service in `docker-compose.yml`) and never ships in the frontend bundle.

### 3. `frontend/src/app/payment/success/page.tsx` (+ `frontend/src/lib/graphql/queries/server.queries.ts`, `frontend/src/lib/graphql/types/stripe.types.ts`)
Query `productKey`. When the session is paid and `productKey=meeting`, show a "Book your session" button linking to Cal.com. The page keeps polling Stripe as it does today and does not read the Payment row.

### 4. Meeting booking email
`backend/src/services/resendMailer.ts`: add a meeting booking sender that reuses the v3.21.1 email layout. From `contact@luisfaria.dev`, reply-to `contact@luisfaria.dev`. Thanks the Supporter and gives the Cal.com link as the CTA.

### 5. `backend/src/services/payments.ts`
On transition to paid with `productKey=meeting`, send the booking email (`sendSupporterEmail` picks coffee thank-you vs meeting booking). Same idempotency guard as the coffee email, so replays don't send it twice. Missing `CAL_MEETING_URL` logs an error and skips the email; the Payment is still recorded.

### 6. Tests
- Backend: `checkoutSessionStatus` returns `productKey`; webhook sends the booking email once for meeting, never for coffee; missing `CAL_MEETING_URL` handled (logged, no crash).
- Frontend: success page shows the button for paid meeting only; hidden for coffee and while unpaid.

## Accepted Risk

Anyone holding the Cal.com link could book without paying. Luis cross-checks bookings against `/admin/payments` by hand. No payment-reference field, no single-use links.

## Edge Cases

- **Paid meeting, never booked:** handled by Luis manually (email the Supporter or refund).
- **Refunds:** only from the Stripe dashboard; the v3.21.1 webhook marks the Payment refunded. A booking made before a refund is cancelled by hand in Cal.com.

## Manual Setup (Luis)

1. Create a Cal.com account and a hidden, free event type for the meeting (e.g. "Consulting session", 60 min). Hidden matters: a public event on the profile can be booked without paying.
2. Set `CAL_MEETING_URL` locally and on the droplet.
