## Overview

The home "Impact at a glance" section was all from the clinics/Konquista era and included two metrics that read as fluffy/hard-to-defend in a technical interview. Curated for freshness + credibility, kept the section (quantified impact is a real differentiator), and moved the data into `content/`.

## Problem

- Every metric was from one past chapter — nothing from the current data role.
- Two were weak: **"+20% Client engagement (GenAI)"** (vague) and **"99.9% Credential theft prevention"** (sounds invented; fails a "how do you measure that?" follow-up).
- Heading "Impact at a glance" doubled the "… at a glance" of the timeline section.

## Changes

- **`frontend/src/content/metrics.ts` (new)** — curated metric groups (single source of truth).
- **`MetricsSection.tsx`** — renders from `content/metrics.ts`; heading "Impact at a glance" → **"Impact"**.
- Dropped the two weak metrics; kept the concrete ones (30K+ msgs, 1M+ records, 20+ clinics, hours saved, 50% email cost, +15% ROI); **added a current data-role metric: ~4 hrs saved per term rollover** (St Catherine's reporting).
