## Overview

Feedback from Luis after using the v3.26 page:
- The pin popup was busy: category, payment and notes for every visit.
- In dark mode its text vanished.
- Amounts were always on show.
- Could the popup bring Google's stars and pictures?

## Problem

- **Busy popup:** `RelationshipMap.tsx` rendered every field of every visit inside Google's `InfoWindow`.
- **Dark mode:** Google always draws the bubble white, while the content inherited the site's light text, so it became light on white. Google also forces `overflow: scroll` on the content box, which draws light scrollbar tracks.
- **Amounts:** shown in three places (total pill, timeline, popup), with no way to hide them.
